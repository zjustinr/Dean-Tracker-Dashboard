Poets & Quants (poetsandquants.com) — Excellent coverage of business school news, including dean appointments, transitions, and "breaking" hires at top programs (e.g., recent stories on Terry College, Haas, Stern, etc.). They often publish dedicated articles when big-name deans are named.
Yutori or similar higher-ed appointment trackers — Aggregates US higher education leadership changes, including business deans.
University newsrooms / official press releases — Most announcements start here (e.g., "University X Names New Dean of School of Business").

3. Social Media & Professional Networks (Best for "Live" Feeds & Real-Time Buzz)
Announcements often break first or get amplified here with celebratory posts, congrats from colleagues, and shared press releases:

LinkedIn — The #1 spot for these hires. Search for phrases like "named dean," "appointed dean," or "new dean of [School of Business]." Many deans, provosts, and universities post directly, and you'll see reshared announcements from recent examples (e.g., Shalei Simms at SUNY Old Westbury, Brian Brown at VCU, Sarah Soule at Stanford GSB). Follow relevant people/companies or set alerts.
X (Twitter) — Use advanced search for real-time posts. Good queries:
("business school" OR "school of business") (dean) (named OR appointed OR "new dean")
Recent examples include announcements about Georgia Terry College and Adam Smith Business School. Follow accounts like @Assoc_of_MBAs, university handles, or higher-ed journalists for quicker visibility.
Facebook & Instagram — Universities sometimes post official announcements or short videos/reels about new deans.

4. Other Useful Spots

Business School Jobs (businessschooljobs.com) — Niche board focused on business education roles.
AGB Search or executive search firm sites — They sometimes publish placed candidates after searches conclude.
Direct university websites — Once a hire is made, it's usually featured prominently on the business school's "About" or "News" page.
