01Editorial

The State of the Bench, 2026

Two lines moved against each other this year, and both are six years in the making. Roughly one in five new appointments was an interim installation in 2023; so far in 2026, it's roughly one in three — the steepest climb we've logged since we started tracking appointment origin this closely. The share of new appointments going to a woman ran the opposite way: it rose through 2023, then reversed, and has fallen every year since.

Interim share of new appointments Held by a woman

By year an appointment began, across all 21 tracked indices. *2026 is partial, through Aug 27.

The interim climb isn't concentrated in one corner of the org chart. Education (11.3% of sitting deans), Creative Arts (9.2%) and the Graduate College (6.8%) show it about as often as the much larger Administrative index does (6.5% of 4,473 sitting officers) — different disciplines, same reluctance to name a permanent successor before a search has fully run its course. Of the 620 appointments logged so far in 2026, 190 — nearly a third — were interim.

The rest of this issue is the board itself: the season's confirmed moves, the stories still developing, the seats still open, and — new this edition — a filterable slate of all 620 tracked appointments. Slice it by track, origin, gender or region below.
