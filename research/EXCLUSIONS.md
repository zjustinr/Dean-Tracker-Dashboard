## Air Force Institute of Technology, Graduate School of Engineering and Management
Dropped 2026-07-24. A federal military graduate school (Wright-Patterson AFB, OH) whose chief executive is titled Director and Chancellor and is an assigned US Air Force officer, not a recruitable academic executive. It is not a market any academic search firm serves, so it is out of scope for this index, on the same reasoning that excludes community-college-only systems. Recorded here so the count is explainable.
