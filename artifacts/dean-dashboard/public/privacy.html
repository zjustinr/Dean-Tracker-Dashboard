Baton Index

Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026

Baton Index ("Baton Index," "we," "us") provides leadership succession data for higher education to a limited set of search firms and institutions under invitation-only pilots and paid access. This policy explains what we collect when you use the product and why.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you or your firm provide when requesting access — name, email, firm name.
  • Access credentials — the access link or code issued to your firm, and a session cookie that keeps you signed in.
  • Usage activity while you're signed in with an access link, including which datasets you open, search terms you enter (for example, a person's name, a school, or a keyword), and candidate lists you build or export within the product.
  • Technical data — IP address, timestamp, and basic request metadata, logged for security and support.

The free public tier does not require an account; anonymous browsing on that tier is metered locally in your browser and is not tied to your identity.

How we use it

  • To operate the product and enforce the access scope your firm has been granted.
  • To understand how a pilot or subscription is being used, so we can support your evaluation and improve the product.
  • To detect misuse, prevent unauthorized sharing of access links, and maintain security.
  • To communicate with you about your trial, account, or the service.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who help us run the product — hosting and infrastructure (Vercel), and email delivery for account communications — under confidentiality obligations, and only to the extent needed to provide the service.

Retention

Usage activity is retained for as long as reasonably needed to support your pilot or subscription and for a limited period afterward for security and record-keeping, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Contact us if you'd like your activity data deleted sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us what data we hold about your firm's usage, request a copy, or request deletion, by emailing us below. If your firm's access is part of an active engagement, some data may be retained as part of that business relationship.

Security

Access links are signed and time-limited. We use industry-standard hosting infrastructure and restrict access to usage data to Baton Index personnel who need it to operate the product.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
