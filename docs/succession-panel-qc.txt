What the rebuild newly revealed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The provost index is the worst-maintained in the corpus, and folding it in
  surfaced that for the first time. 40 of 161 provost seats carry more than one
  sitting provost (Cornell has five) and 39 carry overlapping permanent spells.
  Those rows were in the corpus all along; exiling provosts to their own file in
  the previous export meant no check ever ran across them. They are reported here
  rather than resolved, per the spec: closing a spell needs a source, not a guess.

  Cross-index seat merging recovered 201 interim-to-permanent conversions,
  against 144 in the previous export. Sarah Cole's Columbia conversion is among
  the newly visible ones: her interim spell came from the arts index and her
  permanent spell from creative-arts, so no check could see them as one seat.
